Privacy Policy

How Cryo Divide, its advertising partners and Unity engine diagnostics handle data, and how players control advertising at any time from inside the game.

01The short version

  • Campaign progress, language, age band and advertising choices are stored locally on the device.
  • Adult players may receive LevelPlay/Unity Ads and choose personalised or non-personalised advertising and whether to opt out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising.
  • When “under 18” is selected, the current version does not initialise the advertising SDK and shows no ads.
  • Unity may collect essential technical diagnostics including installation identifiers, device and software information, performance, errors and crashes.
  • The game requires no account, no sign-in and no permission to contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

02Who is responsible

Cryo Divide is developed and published by Goethe47. Goethe47 is responsible for the processing of personal data for which it determines the purposes and means. Unity and the advertising providers may process certain data as processors or as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Contact: burteloffluther@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Android app published as com.goethe47.cryodivide and builds distributed through Google Play testing or release tracks.

03First-launch choices

Before advertising is initialised, the game asks for a language and a neutral age band: under 18 or 18 and over. We do not request or store a birth date. The age band is used only to configure services on that installation.

Adult players choose ad personalisation and whether to opt out of sale/sharing for advertising. Both choices can be changed at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game, and the change is applied to the advertising SDK immediately. Clearing app data also removes them. Material changes will trigger the flow again.

04Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads as the network. Formats are a banner during play, an interstitial after a battle ends, and an optional rewarded video the player may start to double the points earned in that battle.

Depending on choice and region, providers may process the Android advertising ID or other resettable IDs, IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device/OS/app information, ad impressions and interactions, reward completion, diagnostics and fraud/security signals. Purposes include ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, reporting, security and fraud prevention.

Personalised ads can use identifiers and interactions to infer interests. Non-personalised ads are primarily contextual, but limited data may still be processed for delivery, frequency, reporting and security.

Rewarded video is never required to progress. Declining or closing it costs nothing but the doubled reward.

Read Unity's Privacy Policy and LevelPlay privacy information.

05Sale or sharing for advertising

Goethe47 does not sell personal information for money. Some laws define disclosure to ad partners for cross-context behavioural advertising as “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. Adult users may choose Do not sell or share; the game forwards this choice to LevelPlay and disables personalised advertising.

Service-provider transfers may still occur for contextual delivery, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance and aggregate reporting where permitted.

06Unity engine diagnostics

Unity engine diagnostics may automatically collect essential technical data needed for stability and performance. Depending on Unity configuration, this can include an installation ID, session metadata, device model, OS/game versions, locale or coarse region, runtime performance and responsiveness, errors, exceptions, crashes and ANRs.

It supports security, compatibility, performance and stability, and is not used to build a profile of the player. We do not add names, email addresses or custom personal metadata to reports. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

08Children and teenagers

The neutral age screen does not encourage selecting an older age. If “under 18” is selected, LevelPlay is not initialised and no ads are displayed in the current version.

Parents or guardians may contact us. Clearing app storage immediately removes the locally held age choice and progress.

09Legal bases, retention and security

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on consent. Non-personalised delivery and essential diagnostics may rely on consent, contract or legitimate interests depending on provider and region. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in Settings → Privacy; withdrawal applies from that moment onward.

Providers retain data under their policies and legal duties. Cryo Divide does not intentionally transmit personal data over unencrypted connections; data sent by the integrated Unity and advertising SDKs is protected in transit by the security mechanisms those services provide. No system is risk-free.

10Your rights

Depending on location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising. A Unity installation ID or ad-provider ID may be required to locate third-party records.

  • Change ad personalisation or do-not-sell/share at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game.
  • Reset or delete Android's advertising ID in system privacy settings.
  • Clear Cryo Divide app data to erase local progress and choices.
  • Uninstall to remove local storage.
  • Use Unity's privacy tools for data controlled by Unity.

Contact the developer
