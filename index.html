Privacy Policy

01The short version

  • Campaign progress, language, age band and privacy choices are stored locally.
  • Adult players may receive LevelPlay/Unity Ads and choose personalised or non-personalised advertising and whether to opt out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising.
  • When “under 18” is selected, the current version does not initialise the advertising SDK and shows no ads.
  • Unity may collect essential technical diagnostics including installation identifiers, device and software information, performance, errors and crashes.
  • Developer-defined gameplay analytics is not connected. Its separate first-run choice is voluntary.

02Who is responsible

Cryo Divide is developed and published by Goethe47, the controller for the processing described here. Contact: burteloffluther@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Android app and builds distributed through Google Play testing or release tracks.

03First-launch choices

Before advertising is initialised, the game asks for a language and a neutral age band: under 18 or 18 and over. We do not request or store a birth date. The age band is used only to configure services on that installation.

Adult players choose ad personalisation, whether to opt out of sale/sharing for advertising, and whether to allow future gameplay analytics. Clearing app data removes these local choices. Material changes will trigger the flow again.

04Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads. Formats may include banners, interstitials and optional rewarded videos.

Depending on choice and region, providers may process the Android advertising ID or other resettable IDs, IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device/OS/app information, ad impressions and interactions, reward completion, diagnostics and fraud/security signals. Purposes include ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, reporting, security and fraud prevention.

Personalised ads can use identifiers and interactions to infer interests. Non-personalised ads are primarily contextual, but limited data may still be processed for delivery, frequency, reporting and security.

Read Unity's Privacy Policy and LevelPlay privacy information.

05Sale or sharing for advertising

Goethe47 does not sell personal information for money. Some laws define disclosure to ad partners for cross-context behavioural advertising as “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. Adult users may choose Do not sell or share; the game forwards this choice to LevelPlay and disables personalised advertising.

Service-provider transfers may still occur for contextual delivery, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance and aggregate reporting where permitted.

06Unity engine diagnostics

Unity engine diagnostics may automatically collect essential technical data needed for stability and performance. Depending on Unity configuration, this can include an installation ID, session metadata, device model, OS/game versions, locale or coarse region, runtime performance and responsiveness, errors, exceptions, crashes and ANRs.

This is separate from gameplay analytics and supports security, compatibility, performance and stability. We do not add names, email addresses or custom personal metadata to reports. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

07Optional gameplay analytics

Cryo Divide currently sends no developer-defined gameplay analytics. First launch includes a separate voluntary choice for a future feature intended to measure tutorial completion, battle outcomes, performance ranges and balance. Declining does not restrict gameplay or rewards.

No such event will be sent until an implementation exists, this policy and Google Play Data safety name the actual provider/data, and the stored choice permits it.

09Children and teenagers

The neutral age screen does not encourage selecting an older age. If “under 18” is selected, LevelPlay is not initialised and no ads are displayed in the current version. Optional gameplay analytics is not currently implemented.

Parents or guardians may contact us. Clearing app storage immediately removes the locally held age choice and progress.

10Legal bases, retention and security

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising and optional analytics rely on consent. Non-personalised delivery and essential diagnostics may rely on consent, contract or legitimate interests depending on provider and region. Consent can be withdrawn prospectively.

Providers retain data under their policies and legal duties. Network transfers use provider SDKs and encrypted transport where supported. No system is risk-free.

11Your rights

Depending on location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising. Contact burteloffluther@gmail.com. A Unity installation ID or ad-provider ID may be required to locate third-party records.

  • Reset or delete Android's advertising ID in system privacy settings.
  • Clear Cryo Divide app data to erase local progress and choices.
  • Uninstall to remove local storage.
  • Use Unity's privacy tools for data controlled by Unity.

12Google Play, transfers and changes

Google Play handles downloads, updates, testing enrolment and store/stability statistics under Google's Privacy Policy. Providers may process data outside your country using applicable safeguards.

We will update this page when providers, purposes or categories materially change and request a new in-app choice where required.
